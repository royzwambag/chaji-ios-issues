# Privacy Policy for Cha Ji

**Last updated: 25 May 2026**

Cha Ji is a personal tea brewing journal. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Cha Ji does not collect your personal data, does not track you, and contains no ads or analytics.

## Who we are

Cha Ji is developed by Roy Zwambag. If you have any questions about this policy, create an issue in this repo.

## Data you create in the app

Cha Ji lets you record teas and brewing sessions, including names, types, origins, vendors, ratings, notes, brewing parameters, and an optional link to where you bought a tea. This content is created and stored by you.

This data is saved locally on your device. The developer has no access to it.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud enabled for Cha Ji, your teas and brewing sessions sync through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so they stay up to date across your devices. This data is stored in your own private iCloud account. It is not sent to the developer and the developer cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

If you do not use iCloud, your data simply stays on your device.

## App settings

Your preferences, such as default tea type, label style, sort order, and appearance, are stored locally on your device using the system preferences store. These settings never leave your device except as part of standard device backups you control.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- We do not use third-party SDKs or share data with third parties.
- We do not require an account or a login.

## Links to other sites

If you save a purchase or vendor link to a tea and then tap it, the link opens in your browser. Any website you visit that way is outside Cha Ji and has its own privacy policy.

## Children

Cha Ji does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any tea or brewing session inside the app. Removing the app deletes the data stored on your device. Data synced to iCloud can be managed or removed through your device's iCloud settings.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions or requests: create an issue in this repo.
